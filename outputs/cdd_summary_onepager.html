Commercial Due Diligence: China Prestige Skincare TAM Sizing

Scenario: Hypothetical PE acquisition of a mid-size prestige skincare brand, serviceable geography defined as China's top 10 provinces by 2020 census population.

Headline Range

  • TAM (P10–P90): $11,079,545,223 – $23,235,717,362
  • SAM (P10–P90): $5,342,662,638 – $11,880,272,639
  • SOM (P10–P90): $113,252,138 – $322,990,350

Recommendation

The acquisition-supportable range is the SOM figure above, not the TAM headline — TAM overstates addressable opportunity by roughly 83x once channel access and realistic 3-year market share are applied. Diligence should prioritize stress-testing achievable_share, the single assumption with the largest swing on SOM.
